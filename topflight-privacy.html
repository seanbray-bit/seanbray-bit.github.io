TopFlight Privacy Policy

Last updated 29 October 2026.

TopFlight does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data.

  • There are no accounts or sign-ins.
  • There are no adverts, analytics, tracking or third-party services.
  • Your season, cup and settings are stored only on your device and are never sent anywhere.
  • The app does not use the camera, microphone, location, contacts or any other personal information.
