My Flights Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

My Flights is a personal flight tracker for iPhone. No account is required, and the developer does not operate a server that stores your flights.

Information My Flights Stores

My Flights stores the following information on your device:

  • Flights you add: flight number, date, airline, airports, times, aircraft details and status
  • Notes you write for a flight
  • Your settings, such as the font, distance units and alerts
  • Your own FlightAware AeroAPI key, if you choose to add one. It is kept in the iPhone Keychain.

My Flights does not use advertising or third-party analytics SDKs and does not track you across apps or websites.

Flight Lookups

When you add or refresh a flight, My Flights sends the flight number, and the aircraft's identifier once it is known, to these services to get the route and the live position:

  • ADSBdb
  • adsb.lol
  • adsb.fi
  • OpenSky Network

If you add your own FlightAware AeroAPI key, My Flights also sends the flight number, the dates and your key to FlightAware to get times, gates, terminals and delays.

No account, name or list of your flights is sent with a lookup. As with any internet service, these providers can see the internet address the request comes from, and they handle requests under their own privacy terms.

Maps

My Flights shows routes on Apple Maps. Apple may process the map area needed to draw the map under Apple's privacy policy. My Flights does not ask for or use your location.

Alerts

Alerts are created on your iPhone when the app checks your flights. My Flights does not use a push notification server.

Sharing

My Flights shares a flight's details only when you tap Share and choose where to send them. After that, the app or person you chose handles the shared text under their own privacy terms.

Retention and Deletion

Your flights and notes stay on your device until you delete them in My Flights or remove the app. You can remove your FlightAware key in Settings at any time. Because the developer does not receive or store your flights, there is no developer-held account data to request for deletion.

Children

My Flights is a general-audience utility and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

This policy may be updated when My Flights' features or data services change. The current version and update date will remain available on this page.
