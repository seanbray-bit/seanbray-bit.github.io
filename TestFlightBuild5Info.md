# TestFlight Build 5 Information

## Version

- App version: `1.0`
- Build number: `5`
- Date prepared: 2 June 2026
- Upload status: uploaded to App Store Connect and processing

## What Changed

Build 5 expands the Basic Squad section across the Teams area.

### Added

- 37 currently available confirmed World Cup squads.
- 961 player records.
- Player number, name, position, and club shown in each squad row.
- Empty squad message remains for countries whose confirmed public squad was not available in the checked source.

## TestFlight Beta Details

### What To Test

- Open the Teams tab.
- Open England, Brazil, Mexico, USA, France, Germany, Spain, Uruguay, and a few other teams with squads.
- Confirm squad lists scroll smoothly and player rows are readable.
- Confirm player numbers, names, positions, and clubs display properly.
- Open a team without confirmed squad data and check the empty squad message.
- Check the Home countdown and Home Screen widget still work.
- Browse Fixtures, Groups, Venues, and Guide.
- Add a fixture to Calendar.
- Create a Reminder for a fixture.

### Beta App Review Information

The app does not require an account. No login, subscription, payment, special hardware, or server setup is needed.

Suggested review steps:

1. Open the app.
2. Open the Teams tab.
3. Open a team with a squad, such as England or USA.
4. Review the Basic Squad player list.
5. Browse Fixtures, Groups, Venues, and Guide.
6. Test Calendar and Reminder buttons from a fixture.
7. Add the Home Screen widget and confirm the next match countdown appears.
