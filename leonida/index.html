App support

Need help with Neon Coast Companion or spotted an error in the guide? Email Sean Bray, the app’s developer.

sean_bray@sky.com

Report an issue

Include the tab you were using, what you expected, and what happened. For a content correction, include the place, cheat, article, or video title and a link to the source if you have one.

Refresh the guide

Open Home and tap the refresh arrow, or pull down in News, Videos, or Cheats. If a source is unavailable, the app can show previously saved information until you reconnect.

Find your saved places

Open Map and tap the star on a region or place. The regional map marks the regions containing your saved places, and Home lists the places by name. Tap a star again to remove it. For street-level detail, use “Official map on Rockstar’s website” in Map.

About the guide

Neon Coast Companion is Sean Bray’s independent fan guide to Grand Theft Auto VI. For help with a Rockstar account or the game itself, visit Rockstar Games Support.
