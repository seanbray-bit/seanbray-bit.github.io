App support

Need help with Leonida Guide or spotted an error in the guide? Email Sean Bray, the app’s developer.

sean_bray@sky.com

Report an issue

Include the tab you were using, what you expected, and what happened. For a content correction, include the place, cheat, article, or video title and a link to the source if you have one.

Refresh the guide

Open Home and tap the refresh arrow, or pull down in News, Videos, or Cheats. If a source is unavailable, the app can show previously saved information until you reconnect.

Find your saved places

Tap the star on a place in Map. Your starred places also appear on Home. Tap the star again to remove a place.

About the guide

Leonida Guide is Sean Bray’s independent fan guide to Grand Theft Auto VI. For help with a Rockstar account or the game itself, visit Rockstar Games Support.
