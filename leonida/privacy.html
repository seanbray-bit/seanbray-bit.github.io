Privacy policy

Effective 20 November 2026

You can use Neon Coast Companion without an account. Sean Bray, the developer, does not operate a user database or use advertising or analytics SDKs in the app.

Information on your iPhone

The guide stores starred places, downloaded guide entries, and the time of the last refresh on your device. Searching the map and cheat catalog happens on your device. Copying a cheat sequence puts it on the system clipboard. You can remove individual stars in Map. Deleting the app from your iPhone removes its local data.

Content providers and external links

The app requests news, video listings, and cheat information directly from Rockstar Games, YouTube, PC Gamer, PowerPyx, and GTA6Database. The regional map and illustrations are included with the app. Those services receive connection information, including your IP address, when delivering content. Their own privacy policies govern their handling of these requests.

Opening a video, a preset YouTube guide search, a news article, or Rockstar’s map poster takes you to the provider’s website or app. Activity there is subject to that provider’s policy and any account settings you use with it.

Support email

If you contact Sean, your email address and the information you choose to send are used to respond to your request. To ask about or request deletion of a support message, email sean_bray@sky.com.

This website

GitHub Pages hosts these support pages. GitHub processes requests to serve the pages under its privacy statement. Changes to this app policy will appear here with an updated effective date.
