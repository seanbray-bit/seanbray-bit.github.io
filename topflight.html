TopFlight

A fast, pick-up-and-play football game for iPhone and iPad in the classic 16-bit isometric style: a full season, a knockout cup, quick matches and penalty shootouts. No adverts, no in-app purchases, no account. All clubs and players are fictional.

Support

Controls: left of the screen, a stick to move. Right of the screen: tap for a short pass, flick to pass, hold then release for a high ball, and a hard flick towards goal to shoot. Your goalkeeper looks after himself. Game controllers work too: stick to move, A to pass, B to shoot, X to slide.

For help or to report a problem, email the developer, Sean Bray, at sean_bray@sky.com.
