# TestFlight Build 4 Information

## Beta App Description

World Football Tracker is an unofficial tournament companion for following the 2026 football competition. Browse fixtures, groups, teams, venues, match times, knockout rounds, stadium schedules, and simple squad information.

Build 4 adds a basic squad section to team pages, starting with the USA 26-player squad. Each player row shows shirt number, name, position, and club.

This app is not affiliated with FIFA or the tournament organisers.

## What To Test

Please test the new build 4 squad feature:

- Open the Teams tab.
- Open USA.
- Check the Basic Squad section.
- Confirm player numbers, names, positions, and clubs are readable.
- Open other teams and check that the empty squad message is clear.
- Check the Team page still scrolls smoothly.

Also continue testing:

- Home screen countdown timer.
- iPhone Home Screen widget.
- Fixtures, Groups, Teams, Venues, and Guide tabs.
- Calendar and Reminder buttons on fixture cards.
- Closing and reopening the app.
